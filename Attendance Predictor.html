⚡ One-tap import from your ERP

  1. Tap Copy bookmarklet code (one-time setup).
  2. Bookmark any page, name it Get attendance, edit the bookmark's URL and paste the code.
  3. Log in to erp.vce.ac.in, open the Attendance page, then open the bookmark (in Chrome, type its name in the address bar and pick it).
  4. It copies your table and brings you back here. Tap Paste from clipboard.
The bookmark runs inside your logged-in ERP tab, so your password never touches this page.

Or paste manually

Open your portal's attendance page, select the table, copy, then paste here. Data stays in your browser.
